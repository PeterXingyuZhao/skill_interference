# Audit of major experiments against the previous GitHub version

Comparison date: November 6, 2026 (America/Chicago). The live repository was checked before this update. Its only advertised branch was `main`, at [`5993a39`](https://github.com/PeterXingyuZhao/skill_interference/commit/5993a39db0ce5fd5e6278a538327e31fb831a9e3), dated June 25, 2026; no tags were advertised.

## What was already in GitHub

The previous revision had the paper-reproduction framework, earlier animal/number and representation analyses, concept-vector/steering work, diagnostic scripts, and notebooks. It had no recent experiment index, no `results_for_presentation` package, and no modern `docs` catalogue. The recent result bundles below were absent. Existing reproduction code is preserved in this update.

## Work not presented as a completed result

- The red+rectangle B07 neutral-answer comparison is prepared and funded but has no replies at this snapshot. The training curves are real, partial results; prospective fruit/badge/toy/sticker answers are not invented.
- Circle-only training was intentionally discarded before completion.
- Capacity searches, setup failures, archive helpers, and cancelled jobs that never trained are operational history, not additional scientific experiments.
- Unexecuted proposals and reconstructed notebooks without verified student outcomes are not result claims.
- Adjacent confidence-ring work concerns a separate project and was deferred because its referenced raw reproduction bundle was not located in the bounded audit.

## Verification scope

This was a saved-artifact and provenance audit, not a fresh rerun of all experiments. We checked selected immutable source bindings, completed/partial statuses, catalogue links and figure existence. The atlas and cross-model embedded matrices were checked for finite 30×30 shape and labels. The current Metric 1 source bindings and within-prompt normalization were inspected; this update does not claim a fresh recomputation of every original probability cache.

Portable chapters include selected plots, compact data and source hashes. They are a results snapshot for following the work, not a complete training-environment or model-weight release. Operational receipts, machine-specific paths, credentials, provider account details, and large checkpoints are excluded.
